Zone.01 // Summary

Securing complex operational environments

I specialise in Operational Technology cybersecurity across complex, safety-critical and highly regulated environments, with particular experience in the rail sector. I work directly with asset owners, operators and engineering teams to close the gap between regulation and what's actually deployable on live systems.

My work spans cybersecurity strategy, OT cyber risk and maturity assessments, architecture review, incident response and business continuity planning, security assurance and advisory. My approach is risk-based and pragmatic, translating frameworks and regulatory requirements such as IEC 62443, CLC/TS 50701, ISO 27001 and the UK NIS Regulations into security measures that work within real operational environments.

I hold dual master's degrees in Forensic Science and Cybersecurity & Forensics and am an ISA/IEC 62443 Cybersecurity Fundamentals and Risk Assessment Specialist. I am also a Board Member of the ISA UK Section, having previously served as its Cybersecurity Chair.

05

Security Architecture Review

I review IT and OT system and network architectures to understand system boundaries, trust boundaries, network segmentation, interfaces, data flows and the security controls protecting critical functions. I assess the architecture against secure-by-design and defence-in-depth principles, including the zone-and-conduit and system-security concepts in IEC 62443-3-2 and IEC 62443-3-3, NCSC Secure Design Principles and, where appropriate, Zero Trust principles. The review identifies architectural weaknesses, unnecessary trust relationships and control gaps, and provides proportionate recommendations to reduce attack paths, limit the impact of compromise and strengthen the overall security posture.
